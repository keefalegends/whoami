# DESIGN.md - Keefa._OS Design Direction

## 1. Identity & Personality
* **Name & Role:** Keefa Youra Pambudi — Full-Stack Developer & DevOps / Linux Enthusiast based in Indonesia.
* **Core Motif:** Frutiger Aero / Web 2.0 Aqua OS. Crisp aquatic gradients, clean skeuomorphic glass accents, and classic desktop computing nostalgia (Windows XP/Vista era), executed with modern web performance and responsiveness.
* **Tone:** Honest, technical, authentic, friendly, and grounded. Zero corporate buzzwords, zero fabricated metrics, and zero template clichés.

## 2. Palette & Materials
* **Base Atmosphere:** Aquatic sky gradient (`#f0f9ff` to `#bae6fd`), reflecting light and natural water depth.
* **Primary Text:** Deep slate (`#0f172a`) for maximum readability and high contrast (WCAG AA compliant).
* **Secondary Text:** Slate 600 (`#475569`).
* **Accents:** 
  * Aqua / Sky Blue (`#0284c7`, `#38bdf8`) for primary interactive controls.
  * Classic Window Blue (`#1d52d4` to `#123eaf`) for titlebars.
  * Subtle Emerald Green (`#10b981`) for genuine active indicators.
* **Glass & Bevels:** Restrained specular shine highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), clean borders, minimal blur applied only where elevation is needed.

## 3. Typography
* **Headings:** Outfit (Weights: 700, 800, 900) — geometric, rounded curves that match late-2000s computing aesthetics without being bulky.
* **Body & UI:** Inter (Weights: 400, 500, 600) — neutral, highly legible, clean rhythm.

## 4. Dials (antislop specification)
* **ENERGY: 2** — Refreshing and alive with aquatic colors and glossy touches, but restrained from visual chaos.
* **RHYTHM: 2** — Clear visual variety between sections (asymmetric cards, window frames, timeline thread) without repetitive card grids.
* **MOTION: 1** — Functional micro-interactions, smooth hover lifts, and real-time clock. No distracting, constant floating loops.
